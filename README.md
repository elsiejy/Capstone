## Topic selection
#### Option 1: Hospitality Analytics - Hotel Bookings

Problem statement:
How can the hotel use historical booking data to identify seasonal demand fluctuations and booking characteristics associated with cancellations in order to optimise booking strategies, manage demand and reduce booking cancellations?

Research objective:
- Analyse seasonal booking patterns and demand fluctuations - To examine historical hotel booking trends over time and identify seasonality in booking volumes e.g., peak season and off peak period
- Identify the hotel's key customer segments and booking characteristics - To analyse booking data by customer type, market segment, distribution channel, country of origin and other available data points to identify the hotel's major customer segments and understand their booking characteristics
- Analyse booking cancellation patterns and rates - To investigate historical cancellation rates across different time periods, customer segments, market segments and distribution channels to identify groups or periods with higher cancellation rates
- Identify booking characteristics associated with cancellations - To examine the relationships between booking cancellations and relevant booking characteristics, such as lead time, deposit type, customer type, market segment, previous cancellations or requests to determine which factors are associated with a higher likelihood of cancellation
- Recommend data-driven strategies to optimise booking management and reduce cancellations - To develop recommendations based on the findings to improve demand planning, optimise booking strategies and implement targeted measures to reduce cancellations

#### Option 2: Customer Analytics - Telecom Churn Analysis

Problem statement:
How can the telco leverage on historical data to identify key factors associated with customer churn, determine which customer segments are most likely to switch service providers and recommend customer retention strategies to reduce churn.

Research objective:
- To identify the key customer characteristics and factors associated with customer churn using historical customer data - Understand the factors associated with churn e.g., contract type, monthly charges, payment method and service subscriptions
- To identify customer segments with the highest churn rates and the greatest number of churned customers - Distinguish between segments with a high proportion of customers leaving and segments contributing the largest number of churned customers
- To examine the relationship between customer tenure and churn to identify patterns in customer attrition across different stages of the customer lifecycle - Understand whether churn is concentrated among newer customers or occurs throughout the customer lifecycle
- To develop and evaluate a predictive model to identify customers at high risk of churning
- To recommend targeted customer retention strategies based on the findings from the churn analysis and predictive modelling - Translate the analytical findings into actionable strategies for different customer segments and customers identified as being at high risk of churn

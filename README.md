**Choice of project topic**

1. **Hospitality Analytics - Hotel Bookings**
   
   Problem statement: How can historical hotel booking data be analysed to identify seasonal demand patterns, understand booking characteristics associated with cancellations, and develop data-driven strategies to optimise booking management, improve demand planning, and reduce booking cancellations?
   
   Research objective: 
	* Analyse seasonal booking patterns and demand fluctuations - To examine historical hotel booking trends over time and identify seasonal variations in booking volumes, including peak and off-peak periods.
	* Identify the hotel's key customer segments and booking characteristics - To analyse booking data by customer type, market segment, distribution channel, country of origin, and other available variables to identify the hotel's major customer segments and understand their booking characteristics.
	* Analyse booking cancellation patterns and rates - To investigate historical cancellation rates across different time periods, customer segments, market segments, and distribution channels to identify groups or periods with higher cancellation rates.
	* Identify booking characteristics associated with cancellations - To examine the relationships between booking cancellations and relevant booking characteristics, such as lead time, deposit type, customer type, market segment, previous cancellations, and special requests, to determine which factors are associated with a higher likelihood of cancellation.
	* Recommend data-driven strategies to optimise booking management and reduce cancellations - To develop recommendations based on the findings to improve demand planning, optimise booking strategies, and implement targeted measures to reduce cancellations

2. **Customer Analytics - Telecom Churn Analysis**
   
   Problem statement: How can the telco leverage historical customer data to identify key factors associated with customer churn, determine which customer segments are most likely to leave, and develop a predictive model to identify customers at high risk of churning? Based on these insights, what targeted customer retention strategies can the telco implement to reduce its churn rate?
   
   Research objective:
	* To identify the key factors associated with customer churn using historical customer data.
	* To identify customer segments with the highest churn rates and the greatest number of churned customers.
	* To examine the relationship between customer tenure and churn to identify patterns in customer attrition.
	* To develop and evaluate a predictive model for identifying customers at high risk of churning.
	* To recommend targeted customer retention strategies based on the findings from the churn analysis and predictive modelling.

  

3. **Transport & Mobility Analytics - Singapore MRT Analytics**
   Problem statement: How can historical MRT/LRT ridership data be analysed to identify passenger demand patterns, examine variations in station usage over time and identify stations experiencing high or low passenger demand? How can these insights be used to recommend strategies for improving public transport service planning and resource allocation?
   
   Understand overall demand → Identify when demand occurs → Determine where demand is concentrated → Examine how demand changes → Recommend improvements.
   
   Research objective: 
	* Overall ridership trends - analyse ridership trends and patterns over time
	* Peak and off-peak patterns - Identify peak and off-peak periods.
	* Station comparisons - compare ridership across MRT/LRT stations.
	* Changes in station ridership - Identification of changing demand patterns.
	* Recommend strategies to improve public transport service planning and resource allocation.
